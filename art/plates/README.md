# The museum set

Generated for the first pass at the site: photographed Attic red-figure vases,
captioned as catalogue objects. They are genuinely beautiful and they were the
wrong direction — solemn and reverent, asking a reader to admire an object when
the point was to make them smile. A tiny free utility should not present itself
as an exhibit.

Kept because the work is good and may be wanted for something else. Moved out of
`site/` so a deploy does not ship 17 MB nobody looks at.

Regenerate with `python3 art/make.py --only hero` etc. — the prompts live in
`art/make.py` under `STYLE`.
